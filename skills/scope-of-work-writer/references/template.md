# Scope template

Use these headings, in this order. The linter requires all eight.

```markdown
# Scope of Work: <project label>

## Project summary
Two or three sentences: the rooms, the goal, the overall extent.

## Inclusions
- <location>: <action> <material or product level>, <quantity or extent>

## Exclusions
- Items that are not part of this scope, stated plainly.

## Allowances
- <item>: $<amount> allowance. Difference is credited or billed by change order.

## Assumptions
- Site conditions and facts taken as true. If one proves false, a change order follows.

## Schedule
Start window, working days, and what the dates depend on.

## Payment schedule
- <milestone>: <percent>%   (percentages add up to 100)

## Change process
Changes are priced and signed as a change order before the changed work starts.
```

Keep one fact per bullet. Put measurements in the bullet, not in a separate note.
