# Scope of Work: SAMPLE hall bath refresh

All names, quantities and prices in this file are invented sample values.

## Project summary
Refresh one hall bathroom, 8 ft by 5 ft with an 8 ft ceiling. Replace flooring, vanity
and wall finish. The tub and the toilet stay in place.

## Inclusions
- Floor: remove sheet vinyl, install porcelain tile 12 x 24 in, straight lay, 40 sqft
- Walls: hang and finish 8 sheets of 4x8 moisture resistant drywall on walls and ceiling
- Paint: walls and ceiling, two coats, 2 gallons
- Trim: install 24 linear feet of primed baseboard
- Vanity: install one 30 in vanity with top and faucet from the allowances below
- Site: floor protection from the entry to the bath, debris hauled away at completion

## Exclusions
- Permits and permit fees
- Tub, toilet and exhaust fan replacement
- Repair of hidden damage found after demolition
- Testing or removal of hazardous materials

## Allowances
- Vanity with top: $450 allowance. Difference is credited or billed by change order.
- Faucet: $180 allowance. Difference is credited or billed by change order.
- Floor tile: $6 per sqft allowance for 44 sqft. Difference is credited or billed by change order.

## Assumptions
- Subfloor is sound and flat within 1/8 in over 6 ft
- Existing plumbing shutoffs work and supply lines are reusable
- Work area is cleared of personal items before the start date

## Schedule
Six working days from the start date, weekdays between 8 am and 4 pm. Dates depend on
the tile and the vanity being on site before the start.

## Payment schedule
- Deposit at signing: 30%
- After tile is set: 40%
- At completion walk through: 30%

## Change process
Any change to this scope is written, priced and signed as a change order before the
changed work starts.
